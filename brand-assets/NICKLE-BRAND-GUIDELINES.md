## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo (Horizontal)
**File:** `logos/nickle-logo-horizontal.png`

The horizontal logo is the primary brand mark and should be used in most applications.

**Use for:**
- Website headers
- Marketing materials
- Business cards
- Email signatures
- Presentations

**Minimum size:** 120px wide (digital) | 30mm wide (print)

### Vertical Logo
**File:** `logos/nickle-logo-vertical.png`

Use when horizontal space is limited.

**Use for:**
- Mobile app icons
- Square social media profiles
- Vertical banners
- Narrow formats

**Minimum size:** 80px wide (digital) | 20mm wide (print)

### Icon Only
**File:** `nickle-icon-only.png`

Use when the Nickle brand is already established in context.

**Use for:**
- App icons
- Favicons
- Social media avatars
- Small UI elements

**Minimum size:** 32px (digital) | 10mm (print)

### Dark Background Version
**File:** `logos/nickle-logo-dark-bg.png`

Use on dark backgrounds (#1F2937 or darker).

**Use for:**
- Dark mode interfaces
- Dark photography backgrounds
- Night-themed marketing

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

**Nickle Green** (Primary Brand Color)
- HEX: `#22C55E`
- RGB: `34, 197, 94`
- CMYK: `83, 0, 52, 23`
- **Usage:** Primary actions, logos, key highlights, success states

**Dark Gray** (Text & Headers)
- HEX: `#1F2937`
- RGB: `31, 41, 55`
- CMYK: `44, 25, 0, 78`
- **Usage:** Headlines, body text, dark backgrounds

### Secondary Colors

**Blue** (Car/Transport)
- HEX: `#3B82F6`
- RGB: `59, 130, 246`
- **Usage:** Car-related features, transport savings

**Orange** (Expenses)
- HEX: `#F97316`
- RGB: `249, 115, 22`
- **Usage:** Expense categories, warnings

**Purple** (Savings)
- HEX: `#A855F7`
- RGB: `168, 85, 247`
- **Usage:** Savings goals, achievements, success milestones

**Gray** (Supporting Text)
- HEX: `#6B7280`
- RGB: `107, 114, 128`
- **Usage:** Secondary text, captions, metadata

**Light Gray** (Backgrounds)
- HEX: `#F3F4F6`
- RGB: `243, 244, 246`
- **Usage:** Page backgrounds, cards, subtle separators

### Color Usage Guidelines

**DO:**
- Use Nickle Green for primary CTAs and branding
- Use color-coding consistently (Blue = Car, Orange = Expenses, Purple = Savings, Green = Income/House)
- Maintain sufficient contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA minimum)

**DON'T:**
- Don't use colors outside the defined palette
- Don't use Nickle Green on red backgrounds or vice versa
- Don't reduce opacity below 80% for brand colors

## Typography

### Primary Font: **Helvetica / Arial**

**Headlines:**
- Font: Helvetica Bold
- Sizes: 24px - 48px (digital) | 18pt - 36pt (print)
- Color: Dark Gray (#1F2937)
- Line height: 1.2

**Body Text:**
- Font: Helvetica Regular
- Size: 14px - 16px (digital) | 10pt - 12pt (print)
- Color: Dark Gray (#1F2937) or Gray (#6B7280)
- Line height: 1.5

**Captions & Small Text:**
- Font: Helvetica Regular
- Size: 12px - 14px (digital) | 8pt - 10pt (print)
- Color: Gray (#6B7280)
- Line height: 1.4

### Font Hierarchy

```
H1: 36px Bold, Dark Gray
H2: 28px Bold, Dark Gray
H3: 22px Bold, Dark Gray
Body: 16px Regular, Dark Gray
Caption: 14px Regular, Gray
Small: 12px Regular, Gray
```

## Spacing & Layout

### Logo Clear Space
Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the piggy bank icon.

```
    [clear space]
[clear]  LOGO  [clear]
    [clear space]
```

### Grid System
- Use 8px grid for digital designs
- Use 4mm grid for print designs
- Maintain consistent padding: 16px (digital) | 5mm (print)

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Friendly but professional** - "Let's plan your financial future together"
- **Empowering, not patronizing** - "You've got this!" not "You need help"
- **Clear and simple** - Avoid jargon, explain financial terms
- **Optimistic and motivational** - Focus on dreams, not just saving

### Example Messaging

**Good:**
- "Your dream home is closer than you think"
- "Smart budgets meet big ambitions"
- "See exactly when you'll achieve your goals"

**Avoid:**
- "Stop wasting money" (negative)
- "Complex financial optimization algorithms" (jargon)
- "You're bad with money" (patronizing)

## Brand Don'ts

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Rotate or skew the logo
- ❌ Change logo colors outside approved palette
- ❌ Add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines) to logo
- ❌ Place logo on busy backgrounds without clear space
- ❌ Stretch or distort the logo proportions
- ❌ Use old or outdated logo versions
- ❌ Combine logo with other brand marks
- ❌ Use emojis in formal communications (PDFs, contracts)

**DO:**
- ✅ Use approved logo files only
- ✅ Maintain aspect ratios
- ✅ Ensure sufficient contrast
- ✅ Use clear space guidelines
- ✅ Follow color palette strictly
- ✅ Keep designs clean and professional
